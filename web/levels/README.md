# Built in levels

`CCLP1.dat` is Chip's Challenge Level Pack 1 (CCLP1), 149 levels made and chosen by the Chip's Challenge community and released by the Bit Busters Club in 2014 as a free alternative to the original levels.

Its readme says it may be distributed freely, as long as its contents are left intact and unmodified. This copy is unmodified (SHA-256 `46cc0aaa862c7cc5a63aea542eedf86836d6232b1180b3aece64d4de238cae5e`).

Home page and full credits for every level: https://bitbusters.club/cclp1/

The original Chip's Challenge levels (CHIPS.DAT) are not included. Load them from your own copy.
